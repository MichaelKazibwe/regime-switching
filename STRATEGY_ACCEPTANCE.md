**Recommendation-product scope:** The client-facing system now supports recommendation plans and conditional simulations, not account management. Its `PRODUCT_CAPITAL_BANDS_V1_NOT_LEGACY_RISK_BUDGET_AUTHORIZATION` contract uses capital/stress constraints and reports variance attribution; it is separate from the legacy exact-risk-budget order gate. Public advice requires release- and jurisdiction-bound evidence and mandate-specific suitability review via `productrelease.py`. The owner’s prior 50%/three-year/5% objective remains unsupported. Illustrative balanced/growth demo clients are not changes to that mandate or acceptance of higher losses on the owner's account. See [RECOMMENDATION_PRODUCT_GUIDE.md](RECOMMENDATION_PRODUCT_GUIDE.md).

Four additional fixed macro-aware challengers are documented in [FOUR_MACRO_STRATEGIES_ASSESSMENT.md](FOUR_MACRO_STRATEGIES_ASSESSMENT.md). Their 63-session forecasts predict rule-derived macro quadrants, not independently certified recession events. Baseline-relative forecast validation, risk accounting, returns and drawdowns are separate evidence requirements. The owner’s 50%/three-year and 5% drawdown mandate remains unchanged; no portfolio is an accepted optimum merely because four strategies were implemented. Archived pre-feasibility-fix results remain in `var/research/four-macro-strategies-v6/before-budget-feasibility-fix/`; final evaluation uses capped asset-risk budget construction and a no-reserve retry before rejecting a proposal.

**Current owner objective (2026-10-30):** 50% cumulative investment return over three years and a maximum 5% temporary peak-to-trough decline, excluding contributions. This requires approximately 14.47% annual compound growth. The goal study's 12 candidates and 180 fresh three-calendar-year accounts produce **zero joint successes**. No strategy acceptance receipt is issued and no paper activation follows. See [THREE_YEAR_GOAL_ASSESSMENT.md](THREE_YEAR_GOAL_ASSESSMENT.md). Source-hashed local research evidence cannot guarantee a future loss bound, substitute for independent validation, or satisfy future observation gates.

This protocol defines research work that must produce evidence. It does not assert that the existing strategy meets return thresholds.

1. Freeze the 14-ETF universe, source revision, parameter configuration, benchmark hierarchy, and training/validation/holdout date boundaries before evaluation. Record dataset checksums, series IDs, adjustment policy, and licenses/terms. Freeze separate per-series historical FRED releases using `macrovintage.MacroVintageData`; consume `as_of(decision_time)` with an aware clock. The conservative date-only release policy is next New York midnight. Never replace a missing vintage with today's revised series.
2. Reserve the final 20% of available history as an untouched holdout, after ensuring an adequate initial estimation window. Use expanding or rolling chronological training and validation in the earlier history. Fit forecasts, covariance estimates, scalers, calibration, and hyperparameters only on data available at each decision. Avoid random cross-validation across time. Record ETF inception history and selection/survivorship limitations.
3. Generate a decision after its inputs are available; simulate execution no earlier than the next available tradable session. Keep decision prices, execution prices, distributions, and adjusted returns consistent. Report the current worker's adjusted-close fills as operational simulations, not executable historical fills. Model commissions, spread/slippage, turnover and cash reserve; repeat with doubled cost assumptions.
4. Compare net results with SPY, equal weight, a documented static allocation, static risk parity, and cash/T-bills using a consistent calendar. Report CAGR, annualized volatility, Sharpe, Sortino, maximum drawdown, Calmar, expected shortfall, turnover, costs, exposure and factor concentration. Use block-bootstrap confidence intervals and show results by regime and market period.
5. Run ablations for regime detection, expected-return forecasting, covariance estimation, allocation constraints, risk budgeting and volatility targeting. Track every research variant and the selection criterion. Evaluate concentration in equity, duration, inflation, credit and currency factors; test diversification before expanding the universe. Reject changes whose apparent benefit depends on a narrow parameter choice, leakage, one episode or unrealistic costs.
6. Before opening the final holdout, the owner must write numerical return/risk and benchmark-relative acceptance thresholds in a signed research plan. These cannot be inferred from software tests or adjusted after seeing the holdout. The default engineering requirements remain zero unauthorized orders, reconciled accounting, strict risk-budget validation, and repeatable results.
7. Evaluate the holdout once; retain source commit, dataset hashes, train/validation/test boundaries, model fits, net equity curves, metrics, uncertainty, benchmark results, cost sensitivities and limitations. Obtain Michael Kazibwe's review for the pilot; record a PASS or FAIL `strategy_acceptance` receipt with supporting results using `paper_qualification`. A failure requires a new research iteration and a genuinely untouched evaluation policy, not relabeling the old holdout.

Operational paper qualification and strategy acceptance are reported separately. Further return improvements require evidence from this protocol.

The V4 All Weather-informed challenger is also **not accepted**. Its new conditional core/reserve risk contract requires independent validation and runtime authorization integration. Full-history return and drawdown do not jointly improve, the conservative VaR overlay does not meet nominal coverage, and conditional loss frequency does not improve. See [INSTITUTIONAL_PORTFOLIO_REFINEMENT.md](INSTITUTIONAL_PORTFOLIO_REFINEMENT.md). The eight V4 variants bring the inspected smart-variant count to 24, excluding earlier parent/hybrid searches. No new acceptance receipt or production activation follows from this work.
